# Results

- `annotation_summary.json` reports annotation coverage, agreement, and choice summaries for the benchmark.
- `dpo_internvl3_5_4b.json` reports aggregate InternVL3.5-4B DPO evaluation outcomes.
- `dpo_qwen2_5_vl_3b.json` reports aggregate Qwen2.5-VL-3B DPO evaluation outcomes.
- `elo_rankings.json` reports benchmark policy-model Elo rankings.
- `extension_annotation_summary.json` reports annotation summaries for the extension slice.
- `extension_elo_rankings.json` reports Elo rankings for the extension slice.
- `human_policy_baseline.json` reports Elo rankings with a human baseline.
- `imagen_subset_judge_accuracy.json` reports judge accuracy on the Imagen subset.
- `judge_accuracy.json` reports benchmark judge accuracy.
- `judge_length_bias.json` reports judge sensitivity to response length.
- `length_controlled_elo.json` reports the length-controlled Elo analysis.
- `modern_real_image_ablation.json` reports the modern real-image ablation.
- `policy_accuracy.json` reports aggregate policy-model detector accuracy.
- `policy_accuracy_by_source.json` reports policy-model accuracy by image source.
- `qwen7b_cot_ablation.json` reports the Qwen2.5-VL-7B chain-of-thought ablation.
- `response_order_swap.json` reports the response-order swap analysis.
